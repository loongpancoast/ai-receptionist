Questions

Asked by every owner before they switch.

Will customers know they are talking to an AI?
Frontdesk answers in your business's voice and says so honestly when asked. Most customers care about one thing: a fast, correct answer. That is what they get.
What happens when it is not sure?
It does not guess. Anything below your confidence threshold routes to a human with the full conversation attached, and you can set different thresholds for different message types.
Do I keep my existing phone number?
Yes. Frontdesk works with your current business number through SMS, and connects to the Instagram and Google Business accounts you already have.
How long does setup take?
About ten minutes to connect your channels, then an hour or so of training on your pricing, hours, and policies. Most businesses are live the same day.
Can I change the routing rules later?
Anytime, from the dashboard. Add a rule like "weekend emergencies go to the on-call phone" in plain language, no technical work.
